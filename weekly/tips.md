- Live at **https://vamostomar.pt/halloween/** since **Saturday 24 October at 08:00**. Free, in PT/EN/ES, works on phones and computers.
- The idea: Halloween meets **Pão-por-Deus**, the tradition of children going door to door for bread and treats on 1 November.
  You play a little Templar boy or girl and collect the treats the tias hand out before the zombies eat them;
  your sword turns zombies back into neighbours (no blood, for all ages). You survive until the clock runs out, then run to the exit.
- Three rounds in real Tomar places: **1) Praça da República** (the São João Baptista church), **2) the Mouchão park and its water wheel**

  Santa Iria — Café Bistrot** (Rua Marquês de Pombal 57), **offered by the café** (not a paid sponsorship; don't call it one). Voucher valid
  until 30 November 2026, any single item, no alcohol, no cash. Winner announced on **Sunday 1 November** (Dia do Pão-por-Deus) on
  @vamostomarinsta and vamostomar.pt.
- A game of skill, not luck: everyone plays the same rounds with the same treats and zombies. Ages 13+ (13–17 with a parent's or guardian's consent).
  Under-13s can play but can't enter. Always say "competição"/"competition", never "sorteio", "passatempo" or "giveaway".
- **How to enter (put these four steps in the story, in order):**
  1. Play in **Competition** mode at vamostomar.pt/halloween (3 lives, Round 1 to the end).

  4. **One entry per person**: your first post counts. Keep the account public until 8 November; private accounts send the image and caption by DM to @vamostomarinsta.
